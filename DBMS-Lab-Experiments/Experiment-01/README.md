# Experiment 1 – ER Diagram for an Indian E-Commerce Platform

## Aim
Design an Entity–Relationship (ER) diagram for an Indian e-commerce platform.

## Entities
The practical specifies these entities:
- Customer
- Product
- Order
- OrderItem
- Seller
- Category
- Payment
- Delivery
- Address

## Requirements
The ER diagram should identify and represent:
- Primary keys
- Composite attributes
- Multi-valued attributes
- Weak entities
- Specialization of Product
- Participation constraints

## Files to include
- `experiment-01-er-diagram.png` or `experiment-01-er-diagram.pdf` – your completed ER diagram.
- `experiment-01-record.pdf` – practical record, if maintained separately.
- `output/` – any additional diagrams or evidence requested by your faculty.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] All nine specified entities are represented.
- [ ] Primary keys are clearly marked.
- [ ] Composite and multi-valued attributes are shown.
- [ ] Weak entity notation is used where applicable.
- [ ] Product specialization is represented.
- [ ] Participation constraints are labelled.

## Notes
This experiment is a conceptual ER-modelling task. Follow the exact entity and constraint requirements given in the practical sheet.
